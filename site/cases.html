On the production line

These automotive cases show the reach of a welding process problem: supplier traceability, inspection campaigns and, for some defective vehicles, a replacement remedy.

01 / India / 28 October 2024

Volkswagen & Škoda
A suspected missing seam

52cars targeted for recall

14 Kushaq / Slavia + 38 Taigun / Virtus. Financial loss is not stated in the cited register.

What went wrong

SIAM’s manufacturer-submitted register describes a supplier production irregularity: a weld seam on the track control arm may have been omitted. The listed vehicle production window was 29 November 2023 to 20 January 2024.

What was at stake

The two recall entries cover 52 cars. They warn that a component failure during a critical driving manoeuvre could cause abrupt loss of stability and control. The entries describe a suspected defect and a safety risk; they do not establish that a crash occurred.

Relevance to Nabz. This is the closest match to the proposed missing-arc check. With a verified recipe and cycle boundary, an absent arc could trigger a part hold. That is an application hypothesis, not evidence that Nabz would have detected this particular seam.

02 / United States / June 2019

Subaru Legacy & Outback
A process problem at the supplier

2,107vehicles in recall 19V-493

142 Legacy + 1,965 Outback. This is the recall population, not a count of cars replaced or scrapped.

What went wrong

The supplier’s machine for cleaning and reshaping the spot-welder tip could become obstructed with metal debris, reducing welding effectiveness. Improper welds in a duct below the cowl could weaken the body. Subaru’s chronology says a body-shop employee noticed an unusual noise during duct installation on 6 June.

Subaru defect report / cause and chronology, p. 2 ↗

How the loss spreads

Subaru’s records list 2,107 vehicles, including 81 already retailed. The campaign required inspection and holding suspect stock; remedies included factory repair, exchange or repurchase for confirmed defects. Customer support included loaner vehicles and exchange-related fees. The cited records do not provide a total financial loss or final replacement count.

Subaru service bulletin WUH-93R / pp. 1–2 ↗

Relevance to Nabz. Tool condition can turn into downstream inspection and replacement work. This was resistance spot welding; Nabz’s proposed MIG arc and sound rules would need separate development and validation for that process.

03 / United States / September 2018

Jeep Wrangler
The weld was off the seam

18,055vehicles in recall 18V-675

The initial filing estimated 4% had the defect. It reported no known related accidents or injuries as of 19 September 2018.

What went wrong

Some 2018–2019 Wranglers had front track-bar bracket welds positioned away from the intended seam. A bracket could separate from the frame and reduce steering response. The filing identifies a suspect production period from 7 May to 21 August 2018.

How the loss spreads

The recall covered 18,055 vehicles for weld inspection and repair where needed, at no charge to owners. Supplier records and part traceability defined the affected range. Neither cited document puts a monetary total on the campaign.

FCA defect report / population, defect and chronology ↗ NHTSA acknowledgement / remedy ↗

Relevance to Nabz. An arc can fire for the expected duration at the wrong location. Counting arcs alone cannot establish weld placement. Fixture control and geometry inspection remain necessary.

04 / California, United States / 9 September 2010

San Bruno pipeline
A seam flaw left in service

US$220m+estimated property damage

PG&E’s estimate as reported by PHMSA. This is not a total of all penalties, legal costs or subsequent spending.

What went wrong

The NTSB traced the rupture to a poorly welded pipe section installed in 1956. A seam flaw grew until the pipe failed during a pressure increase. Its probable-cause finding includes both deficient construction quality control and an integrity-management programme that failed to find and remove the defective section.

NTSB PAR-11/01 / probable cause, p. xii ↗

The documented loss

The rupture and fire killed eight people and destroyed 38 homes. PHMSA reports PG&E’s property-damage estimate as exceeding US$220 million. The harm extended far beyond the pipeline itself.

PHMSA / consequences and property-damage estimate ↗

The lesson for inspection. A recorded welding operation does not prove a sound internal joint. This case supports rigorous fabrication checks and in-service integrity management; it does not demonstrate that Nabz can detect pipeline seam flaws or prevent such a rupture.

05 / Colorado, United States / 15 October 2023

Pueblo derailment
A mismatched rail weld failed

US$15.6mdamage reported by the NTSB

Accident damage in the final report, issued 12 February 2025. This figure does not put a value on the life lost.

What went wrong

The NTSB found that a thermite weld joined rails with different profiles. Cracking near the weld progressed until the rail broke. Investigators concluded that the welder likely had not used the required kit for joining the mismatched rails; the reason was unknown.

The documented loss

Thirty-one coal cars derailed. Cars struck and partially collapsed a bridge over Interstate 25, killing a truck driver below. The final report lists US$15.6 million in damage. BNSF subsequently added failed-weld analysis and random weld audits.

NTSB RIR-25-03 / damage p. 1; findings and lessons pp. 2–4 ↗

The lesson for process control. The correct procedure and joint preparation matter as much as completing the operation. Thermite welding has no MIG arc to count; this case is outside Nabz’s proposed sensing scope.

How to read the evidence

A reason to inspect earlier.

These records establish the consequences of missed, misplaced or defective welds. They do not measure Nabz’s detection rate or prove that its proposed warning would have prevented an incident.

Figures retain their source currency and category. No recall count is converted into an estimated cash loss, and no total combines the cases. Product-relevance notes are our interpretation. The fictional Station 02 savings example remains a separate, editable model.
